About XVisionary

XVisionary is a small shop that makes beginner-friendly money tools for freelancers and side hustlers. The calculators on this site are free and use plain JavaScript in your browser: no accounts, no cookies, no trackers, and nothing you type leaves your device. Each one shows its formula and a worked example so you can check the math yourself.

If you'd rather work in a spreadsheet, XVisionary also sells a few templates (rate and quote, invoices, tax set-aside, side-hustle profit). Everything on this site works without them. Browse the shop.
